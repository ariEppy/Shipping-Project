# Shipping-Project
This project was written in SQL in MYSQL.

It consists of a database, functions, inserts, and triggers & procedures.

Our database management system allows its users to efficiently store, organize, manage, and retrieve data about our ships and deliveries. We can track your orders in every corner of the world, ensuring that your products will arrive quickly and safely to your homes. This system chooses the right ship, containers, and crew for your product’s journey.
